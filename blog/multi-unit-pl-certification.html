For corporate restaurant teams, the Profit & Loss statement is the foundation of decision-making. But there is a fundamental problem: most P&L data is not verified.

It is aggregated from multiple systems, each with its own assumptions, definitions, and inconsistencies. By the time it reaches corporate leadership, it is often accepted at face value. This creates risk.

P&L certification

FohBoh | MGE™ addresses this by introducing P&L certification. Instead of relying on unverified data, MGE validates every component of the P&L—revenue, labor, cost of goods sold, and vendor charges. Each metric is:

  • Defined in a KPI registry
  • Calculated using deterministic rules
  • Locked and audited
This transforms the P&L from a report into a certified financial instrument.

What it enables

For corporate teams, this enables confidence in financial accuracy, faster decision-making, and reduced risk in reporting. It also supports compliance and governance. Certified P&L data can be audited, defended, and used in external reporting.

This is particularly important as regulatory scrutiny increases. In the future, organizations will not just need to report numbers—they will need to prove them. MGE makes this possible.

Trust as infrastructure

By certifying financial data at the source, it ensures that every downstream analysis is grounded in truth. For multi-unit operators, this represents a shift: from reporting to certification, from insight to proof. Because at scale, trust is not optional. It is infrastructure.
