In a world where AI-generated insights are becoming commonplace, one question matters more than ever: can you prove it? The Court-Admissible Analysis Report (CAAR) is FohBoh.ai's answer. It is not just a report—it is a certified, evidentiary instrument designed to stand up to scrutiny in disputes, audits, and legal proceedings.

Unlike traditional analytics outputs, which often rely on interpretation or probabilistic models, a CAAR is built through a deterministic certification process. Every number, every conclusion, and every statement is derived from a governed, rule-based system designed for traceability and verification.

How a CAAR is produced

At a technical level, CAAR is produced by the FohBoh | MGE™ (Metrics Governance Engine), which executes a structured pipeline of deterministic rules. Each step—from data ingestion to final output—is logged, validated, and secured.

According to the FohBoh MGE Evidentiary Architecture Brief, the system is explicitly designed to align with key legal standards, including Federal Rules of Evidence 803(6), 902(11), and 1002. This means the architecture itself is engineered to support:

  • Business records admissibility
  • Self-authentication without live testimony
  • Preservation of original source data

This is a critical distinction. FohBoh is not acting as a collection agency. It does not pursue claims or negotiate disputes. Instead, it produces a certified analytical artifact that restaurant operators can use at their discretion. The CAAR is an evidentiary tool—not a demand instrument.

Four foundational principles

What makes CAAR unique is how it establishes trust through four foundational principles:

  • Determinism — the system produces identical outputs for identical inputs. No variability. No interpretation drift.
  • Traceability — every finding is linked to specific source records and rule IDs, creating a complete audit trail.
  • Immutability — certified records are locked and hashed using SHA-256, ensuring they cannot be altered without detection.
  • Transparency — the entire transformation, from raw data to certified output, is fully documented and verifiable.
The result is a report that moves beyond "insight" and into proof.

Intelligence you can defend

For restaurant operators, this changes the game. Instead of arguing with vendors, processors, or delivery platforms based on assumptions, they can present a structured, certified analysis backed by verifiable data.

In an era where AI is often questioned for its reliability, CAAR represents a new standard: intelligence you can defend. Because in the end, insights are only valuable if they are trusted—and trust must be proven.
